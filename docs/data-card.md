## Unit of record

A **conversation** is a sequence of messages within one interaction session. One individual can have many conversations. Conversation counts therefore do not equal patient counts.

## Known limitations

- Reported conditions and medications are not confirmed diagnoses or administered treatments.
- People describe the same symptom in many different ways, and in different languages.
- Platform users are not a random sample of the population. Users of digital health tools may differ in age, health literacy and access to care.
- Conversations may be incomplete. A patient may not return, and the absence of a follow-up message does not mean an absence of events.

## Privacy

Conversations can contain direct identifiers (names, contact details) and indirect identifiers (rare conditions, places, dates). Any research extract will require identifier removal, data minimisation and a disclosure-risk assessment suited to the proposed study.
